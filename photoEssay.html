artist bio:

Maya Estrera (she/her) is an award winning documentary filmmaker and photographer dedicated to sharing stories that change people.
She graduated from Swarthmore College with a B.A. in Film & Media Studies and Environmental Studies in 2025 and is currently pursuing
an MFA in Integrated Media Arts at Hunter College. Her most recent project, HYPERSCALED, unearths the real world cost of AI and data centers
in the small community of Bessemer, Alabama. In her free time, she enjoys film photography, DJing, and spending time outdoors.
